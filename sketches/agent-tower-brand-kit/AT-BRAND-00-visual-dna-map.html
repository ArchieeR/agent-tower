06 · Owner review

Three decisions unlock the next sheet

After review, accepted/provisional/rejected dispositions will be recorded in ALD-132 before the token source or icon matrix is promoted.

Q01

Should Signal Plaque become the provisional icon direction?

Alternative: choose Precision Line or Workshop Stamp, or specify the hybrid boundary differently.

Q02

Is the warm physical layer restrained enough?

Current proposal keeps timber, brass, paper and cork contextual; default operational surfaces stay dark enamel.

Q03

Should cyan remain the single global operational signal?

Departments keep limited identity accents; semantic state colours remain green, amber, red, violet and brass.
